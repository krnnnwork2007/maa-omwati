Subsidy and net metering

The savings are real. The paperwork should not put you off.

We help homes apply under the PM Surya Ghar rooftop scheme and set up net metering, so extra units you send to the grid come back as credit.

Usually needed: a recent electricity bill, ID and address proof, and details showing the roof is yours to use.

With net metering you pay only for the difference between what you use and what you send out.

FAQ

Quick answers.

How big a system do I need?

About 120 units a month per kW is a good rule for Delhi. Divide your monthly units by 120 for a starting size. We adjust after seeing your roof.

Is there a government subsidy?

Homes can apply under the PM Surya Ghar rooftop solar scheme. Rules and amounts change, so we confirm them at quote time and help with the application.

Will it run during a power cut?

A standard on-grid system switches off when the grid goes down, for safety. A hybrid system with a battery can keep essentials running.

Do panels need cleaning?

Yes. Delhi dust builds up fast and lowers output. Clean regularly or ask us for a service visit.

How long does installation take?

It depends on size and approvals. We give an expected timeline in the proposal.

Do you work outside Narela?

We work across North Delhi. Call with your location and we will confirm.
